#3 - Wuthering Waves

Background: Wuthering Waves is an action RPG from Kuro Games, released in 2024, set in a post-apocalyptic world. The planet was devastated by a disaster called the Lament, and sound-based monsters now roam the land.

Story: You play as Rover, a person who wakes from a long sleep with no memory and a rare power. Along with the Resonators, people with special abilities, you work to understand the Lament, stop the monster outbreaks, and uncover the truth about your own past.

Review: The combat is fast, stylish, and very responsive, with a dodge and parry system that feels great to master. The visuals and music are polished, and the world is fun to explore. The story starts slow, but it gets much better as you go.
