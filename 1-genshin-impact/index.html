#1 - Genshin Impact

Background: Genshin Impact is a free-to-play open-world action RPG from HoYoverse, released in 2020. It takes place in Teyvat, a fantasy world split into nations that each worship a different element and a different god.

Story: You play as the Traveler, who is separated from their twin sibling by an unknown god at the start of the game. With the help of a floating companion named Paimon, you travel from nation to nation, helping locals and uncovering a larger conspiracy while searching for your sibling.

Review: Genshin’s world is beautiful and full of things to discover, and the elemental combat rewards smart team-building. The soundtrack and character designs are some of the best in gaming. The gacha system can be grindy, but the free content is generous enough to enjoy without spending.
