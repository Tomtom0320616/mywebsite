#2 - Metal Gear Solid V: The Phantom Pain

Background: Directed by Hideo Kojima and released in 2015, this is a stealth action game set in 1984 during the Cold War. It follows the legendary soldier Big Boss.

Story: After nine years in a coma, Big Boss wakes up in a hospital under attack and escapes to build a new private army called Diamond Dogs. His mission is to take revenge on the group that destroyed his old one, and the story leads through Afghanistan and Africa to a twist that reframes everything.

Review: The gameplay is some of the best in the stealth genre, giving you endless freedom to approach each mission your own way. Building your base and recruiting soldiers is very satisfying. The story feels unfinished in places, but the controls and sandbox missions make up for it.
