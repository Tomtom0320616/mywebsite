#5 - Arknights: Endfield

Background: Endfield is a 3D real-time strategy RPG from Hypergryph, set in the same universe as the mobile hit Arknights but on a new frontier planet called Talos-II.

Story: You play as the Endministrator, the leader of the Endfield Industries expedition. Your team sets up outposts, builds automated factories, and fights hostile forces, all while discovering the mysteries of the planet and its dangerous energy source, Originium.

Review: Endfield mixes squad-based combat with factory building, which makes it feel different from other gacha games. The art style and world design are impressive and fit the series well. The factory systems can be complex at first, but they give the game a lot of depth.
