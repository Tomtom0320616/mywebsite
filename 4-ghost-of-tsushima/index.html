#4 - Ghost of Tsushima

Background: Made by Sucker Punch and released in 2020, this open-world action game is set during the Mongol invasion of Tsushima Island in 1274.

Story: You play as Jin Sakai, a samurai who survives the first brutal attack on his island. To defeat the Mongol army, Jin must give up the honorable samurai code he was raised with and fight in secret as the “Ghost,” a feared and mysterious warrior, which puts him in conflict with his own uncle.

Review: This is one of the most beautiful games ever made, with sweeping landscapes, wind that guides you, and cinematic duels. The sword combat feels weighty and satisfying, and the story has real emotional impact. Some side activities are repetitive, but the atmosphere makes it worth every hour.
